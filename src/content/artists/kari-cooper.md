Kari Cooper is an award winning designer,

illustrator and artist based in Waikerie,

in South Australia’s Riverland region.

Her whimsical, narrative driven work

explores folklore, fairytales and the timeless

magic of visual storytelling.

Inspired by the natural world, vintage

illustration, folklore, fairytales and traditions

passed down through generations, Kari’s work

explores the enduring stories and cultural

heritage that connect us across time and place.

Drawing on her background in graphic design

and illustration, Kari combines contemporary

design sensibilities with traditional art

practices, working across ink, watercolour,

gouache, acrylic and oils.

Kari began teaching art in 2023 and began

exhibiting her work in 2024.

She is currently the Creative Director of

Art Folk Atelier, an independent creative studio

and art space located within the Waikerie Collective.
